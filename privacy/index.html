Privacy Policy

Version 0.6 (draft) · Last updated 9 November 2026

Core16 helps high school hockey players and their families keep track of their school work and, for students in Ontario and British Columbia, NCAA Division I and II academic eligibility. Some families come to us directly, and some through their hockey team. To do that we handle information about students, most of whom are under 18. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

In this policy, "Core16", "we" and "us" means [legal name of the business]. "You" means the parent or guardian who holds the account, and the student whose record it is.

1. The short version

  • We collect what we need to track a student's courses and marks against the NCAA rules, the hockey schedule those marks have to fit around, and what we need to advise the family. Nothing else.
  • A parent or guardian gives consent for each student, and we record when and how.
  • We store student records in Canada (Toronto).
  • We don't sell information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • Ask Core16 and video sessions are optional, and stay off without consent. Neither is needed to use the rest of Core16, and refusing either changes nothing else.
  • Ask Core16, our AI assistant, is off for a student until a parent consents and the student agrees too. It sends what's typed to Anthropic in the United States, and can send a student's courses and marks, but not their name, school or birth date. Once a parent consents, our advisors can ask it about that student too. See section 7.
  • A video session with a professional can't be booked until a parent consents. Calls run through Daily, usually in the United States, and are never recorded. See section 8.
  • If we text you a link code, the text contains only the code and a link, and we don't keep your phone number.
  • We use email addresses to sign you in and to work with your family, never to send marketing you haven't agreed to.
  • If a student's hockey team uses Core16, its staff see some of the student's information. Coaches and managers see a short summary of how school is going, never a mark or a course. The team's academic advisor sees the student's file. Notes a coach writes to a player are always the family's to read. See section 10.
  • Students outside Ontario and BC, and families who turn it off, use Core16 without NCAA tracking. We then work out and keep no NCAA figures for that student, and don't ask for a birth month or year.
  • Core16 is not the NCAA. Only the NCAA Eligibility Center can certify a student, and we don't share records with it or with schools on your behalf.

  • Team membership, if the student's hockey team uses Core16: the team and season, jersey number and position, and when they joined and left. See section 10.
  • Coach's notes, if the team uses Core16: a note a coach or manager writes to the player about a game or practice, with every earlier version of it. See section 10.

Created by our advisors

  • Contact log: a dated note of each conversation with the family (who we spoke to, how, and what we advised). It's our record of the advice we gave.
  • Access log: every time a staff member opens a student's record, we log who opened it and when. That includes a team's staff opening a player's page or a game's notes (section 10). We also log when a question asked on a web page reads a student's figures, but not the words. A question asked on the phone isn't logged this way, because a phone isn't tied to one person.

Calls from our advisors

  • Call records: if an advisor phones a parent through Core16, who was called, both phone numbers, how the call went and how long it lasted. Calls aren't recorded. See section 5.

For linking a phone

  • Link codes: a short one-time code our staff give you so the app can connect to the student's record. A code works once and expires after 7 days.
  • Device tokens: once linked, the phone holds a token that lets it sync. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, not the token itself.
  • Web sign-in codes: a short code the phone shows so the student can sign in on the web. We store only a scrambled version, and it works once, for five minutes (section 9).
  • Phone number, if you ask us to text you a code. See section 5.
  • Push address: if you allow notifications on a student's phone, the address Apple gives the app there, so we can update the number on the app's icon when an advisor replies to a question the student sent. That number is all we send this way: no words, and no names.

What we don't collect

We don't collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics, and we don't record video sessions or phone calls. The only photos and health information we hold are the report cards and IEPs a family chooses to send us, described above, and anything someone types into Ask Core16 or a question they send to an advisor. The app doesn't include third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

3. How we use it

  • To calculate and show the student's NCAA academic eligibility status, and to flag problems early, such as a course that doesn't count or a mark just below a grade band. Only for a student with NCAA tracking on.
  • To let our advisors review the student's progress and give advice.
  • If the student's team uses Core16: to show the team's schedule on the family's pages, to let the team's staff see what section 10 lists and write notes to the player, and to let the team's staff talk to each other and to us.
  • To keep a record of what we calculated and advised, so we can explain it later if a question comes up.
  • To restore a student's record if it's accidentally overwritten, for example by an old phone syncing.
  • To manage your account and billing.
  • To answer questions asked through Ask Core16, if you've chosen to use it (section 7).
  • To arrange and run video sessions, if a parent has consented to them (section 8).
  • To sign parents and students in to their web pages (section 9).
  • To send link codes you've asked for, and documents for you to sign.
  • To email you sign-in links and codes, account and billing notices, and messages from your advisor.

We don't use student information for marketing, and we don't sell it, rent it or share it for anyone else's marketing.

7. Ask Core16, our AI assistant

Ask Core16 answers questions about NCAA rules. It's optional, and it's off for a student unless a parent consents and the student agrees too. It runs on Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, so it isn't a person and it can be wrong. It can't certify anything: only the NCAA Eligibility Center can. [Only demo students can use it until counsel has reviewed this section.]

Who has to agree

  • For a student to use it, two people agree. A parent or guardian signs a separate consent, and the student then agrees for themselves. A student can ask us to email the family's address so a parent can sign. [Needs counsel: whether a parent's consent is required, and at what age a student can agree alone.]
  • A parent asking about their child needs only their own signed consent, so a parent can still ask, and their question can still read the student's figures, after the student has turned it off for themselves. [Needs counsel.]
  • Either can stop it at any time. A parent can withdraw consent from their family page, which stops it answering for that student. A student can turn it off in the app or on their page. Either takes effect on the next question.
  • Saying no costs nothing else. The tracker and the family page work the same without it.

What goes to Anthropic

Each question goes through our server to Anthropic, in the United States:

  • Always: what was typed, the conversation so far on that screen, the student's province and today's date.
  • If Ask Core16 needs the student's own figures to answer: each course's code, title, grade, subject, credit value, status, mark and whether it counts; core GPA, its minimum, and credits by subject and by the 10/7 rule; how the student's core courses are pacing; marks close to the next grade band; and the graduation year and the year the five-year eligibility period starts. It fetches these only when a question calls for them, so a question about a rule normally sends none of this. For a student without NCAA tracking there are no figures, and none is sent.
  • Never from our records: the student's name, school, birth date, NCAA ID or assignments.

This isn't anonymous. A province, a graduation year and a full list of courses and marks could together point to one student, the year the five-year period starts narrows down when they were born, and we can't control what someone types into a question or a course title. Please don't type a name, a school or anything private. Ask Core16 may ask for a birth month and year if a question needs them and we don't have them, and anything typed in answer goes to Anthropic too.

Anthropic may keep this for a short time under its own terms. [Confirm from our current agreement with Anthropic: how long it keeps this, and that it doesn't use it to train its models.]

What we keep

  • The conversation: nothing. It stays on the phone's screen until you leave it, or in that browser tab until you close it or sign out. Nobody can read it back later: not you, not your advisor and not Core16, so we hold no record of what Ask Core16 said. [Needs counsel.]
  • How much it's used. We count how many questions a student asks, and how many each parent asks about them, on which days, whether on the phone or the web, and how many read the student's own figures, so the student's advisor can see it's being used. We never keep what was asked. Counting began with the wording that says so.
  • A question you choose to send to an advisor. A button under each answer does this, and it asks first. We then keep that question, its answer and anything you add, but not the rest of the conversation. Every Core16 advisor and administrator can read it.
  • The advisor's reply shows on the screens of whoever asked, and goes in the contact log. A student's question and its reply aren't shown on a parent's page, and a parent's aren't shown to the student. [Needs counsel, and a qualified person: an adult replying in writing to a minor.]
  • A follow-up you write to a reply goes to the advisor and not to Ask Core16, and we keep it the same way.
  • Removing a question from your screens doesn't delete it. We keep a copy for your advisor, because it's part of our record of the advice we gave. To have it deleted, ask us (section 14).

How we tell you about a reply

  • A student sees a number on the app's icon, if they've allowed notifications, and a note in the app and on their page. The number is all that's sent.
  • A parent gets a notification in the Core16 Portal app, if they use it and allow notifications, and an email to the family's address. Neither contains the student's name, the question or the reply.

10. If the student's hockey team uses Core16

A hockey league or team can sign up with Core16 for its players. The team's staff (its coaches, manager, team administrator and academic advisor) then sign in to Core16 themselves, with a passkey or an emailed code, and see their own team's players and no one else's. They see a player only while the player is on the team. [Not for real players until counsel has reviewed this section. Needs counsel: whether the team, the league or Core16 is responsible for each player's information, and whether each team signs a data processing agreement with Core16.]

What a team's staff see

  • Coaches, the manager and the team administrator see each player's name, jersey number, position, grade, province and school, and a short summary of school: whether the student is on track, needs attention or has no record yet; how many assignments are overdue; how heavy the next seven days are, from the kinds of work due (an exam counts for more than a quiz); and the kind and day of the next test, exam, essay or presentation. 11. Where information is stored

On our servers. Student records, eligibility results, the contact log and the access log are stored in a database hosted by DigitalOcean in Toronto, Canada.

On your phone. The app keeps a copy of the student's record on the device so it works offline, and syncs changes to our servers.

Calendar and reminders (optional, off by default). If you turn them on in the app's Profile screen:

  • The app adds upcoming assignments to a separate "Core16" calendar on your phone. It only ever changes that calendar, and doesn't send any calendar information to us.
  • The app schedules assignment reminders on your phone. These are created by the phone itself, not sent from our servers.

You can turn either off in the app, or in your phone's Settings.

14. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you or your student.
  • Correct anything that's wrong. Families can fix most details directly in the app, and our advisors can correct a student's name in our records. Advisor contact-log entries aren't edited. Instead, we add a correction that points to the original entry, and both stay visible, so the record stays accurate.
  • Stop using Ask Core16 without closing anything: a parent withdraws consent from their family page, and a student turns it off in the app or on their page (section 7). This doesn't delete a question already sent to an advisor; ask us if you want that deleted.
  • Stop video sessions without closing anything: a parent withdraws consent on their family page or through their advisor (section 8).
  • Withdraw consent and close the account. We archive the student's record: the app stops syncing, advisors no longer see the student in their day-to-day lists, and a phone can't reconnect without a new link code. The record itself is kept for the periods in section 13, so it can be restored if you come back, unless you also ask us to delete it.
  • Delete a student's information. We permanently delete everything we hold about the student: their profile, courses and marks, earlier versions of the record, eligibility results, the advisor contact log, questions sent to an advisor, video session bookings and attendance, call records, documents signed, team memberships, coach's notes to them, the staff access log entries about them, consent records, link codes, device tokens and push addresses. A team's staff chat isn't searched for the student's name, so a message that mentions them stays: ask us and we'll remove it. [Confirm with counsel whether removing is enough, since a removed message is still stored.] If they're the only student on the account, we also delete the account, including the parent or guardian's email address, the family's list of parents and their sign-ins, billing status and consent records. This includes the record of advice we gave, so we can't later show what we told you. [Confirm with counsel.]

Once deleted, information can't be recovered by us. Two things remain: our staff access log keeps an entry recording that a student's information was deleted, when, and by whom, identified only by an internal number with no name or other details; and deleted information can stay in our hosting provider's database backups until they expire (section 13). The app keeps its own copy on your phone until you delete it there or remove the app.

To make a request, contact us (section 17). We'll answer within 30 days, and may need to confirm your identity and your relationship to the student first.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) [or the BC Information and Privacy Commissioner, if BC's PIPA applies; the Alberta Information and Privacy Commissioner, if Alberta's PIPA does; the Commission d'accès à l'information for a Quebec student; and whatever applies to a student in Washington state: confirm with counsel].

15. How we protect information

  • Records are hosted in Canada and encrypted in transit between the app, the advisor portal and our servers.
  • No passwords: staff and parents sign in with passkeys, parents and a team's staff can also use a one-time code we email, and phones and students connect with one-time codes and device tokens. We store every code and token only in scrambled (hashed) form. Core16's own staff can't sign in by email.
  • Signed-in sessions expire, and every staff view of a student's record is logged.
  • Only Core16 advisors and administrators can see whole student records across families, and there are no shared logins. A team's academic advisor sees the files of their own team's players while they're on it, and the rest of a team's staff see only what section 10 lists. A parent sees their own family's students and a student sees their own. A professional sees only what section 8 lists. Ask Core16 sends only what section 7 lists.
  • If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll notify the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner, as the law requires.

No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there's less to protect.
