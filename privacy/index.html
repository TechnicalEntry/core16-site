Privacy Policy

Version 0.2 (draft) · Last updated 17 September 2026

Core16 helps Canadian high school hockey players and their families keep track of NCAA Division I and II academic eligibility. To do that we handle information about students, most of whom are under 18. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

In this policy, "Core16", "we" and "us" means [legal name of the business]. "You" means the parent or guardian who holds the account, and the student whose record it is.

1. The short version

  • We collect what we need to track a student's courses and marks against the NCAA rules, and to advise the family. Nothing else.
  • A parent or guardian gives consent for each student, and we record when and how.
  • We store student records in Canada (Toronto).
  • We don't sell information, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools in the app.
  • If we text you a link code, the text contains only the code and a link, and we don't keep your phone number.
  • We use email addresses to sign you in and to work with your family, never to send marketing you haven't agreed to.
  • Core16 is not the NCAA. Only the NCAA Eligibility Center can certify a student, and we don't share records with it or with schools on your behalf.

2. Information we collect

About the parent or guardian

  • Email address, to identify the account, sign you in and stay in touch. Core16 doesn't use passwords. See section 6.
  • Billing status: whether the account is paid, invoiced or complimentary, and the reference numbers our payment processor gives us. Card details go to the payment processor and never reach us. [Billing isn't set up yet; confirm before publishing.]
  • Consent records: which policy version you agreed to, for which student, when, and how.

About the student

  • Email address, only if the student signs in to the app themselves. See section 6. [Student sign-in isn't built yet.]
  • Profile: first and last name, province, grade, school, graduation year, sport, and the NCAA division being targeted.
  • NCAA ID, if you enter one. It's optional. BC families are asked to use it as the answer to the BC Ministry of Education transcript security question, and we remind them to.
  • Courses and marks: course codes and titles, credit values, whether a course is finished or in progress, marks entered from report cards (percentages, or proficiency levels for BC grade 9) and the date of each report card.
  • Assignments: title, due date, weight and score, if the family chooses to log them.
  • Eligibility results that our server calculates from the above, such as core course count, core GPA, courses that don't count, and marks close to the next grade band. Each result records which version of the NCAA rules produced it.

Created by our advisors

  • Contact log: a dated note of each conversation with the family (who we spoke to, how, and what we advised). It's our record of the advice we gave.
  • Access log: every time a staff member opens a student's record, we log who opened it and when.

For linking a phone

  • Link codes: a short one-time code our staff give you so the app can connect to the student's record. A code works once and expires after 7 days.
  • Device tokens: once linked, the phone holds a token that lets it sync. We store only a scrambled (hashed) version of it, not the token itself.
  • Phone number, only if you ask us to text you a code. See section 5.

What we don't collect

We don't collect location, contacts, photos, health information, advertising identifiers, or usage analytics. The app doesn't include third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools.

3. How we use it

  • To calculate and show the student's NCAA academic eligibility status, and to flag problems early, such as a course that doesn't count or a mark just below a grade band.
  • To let our advisors review the student's progress and give advice.
  • To keep a record of what we calculated and advised, so we can explain it later if a question comes up.
  • To restore a student's record if it's accidentally overwritten, for example by an old phone syncing.
  • To manage your account and billing.
  • To send link codes you've asked for.
  • To email you sign-in links, account and billing notices, and messages from your advisor.

We don't use student information for marketing, and we don't sell it, rent it or share it for anyone else's marketing.

5. Text messages (SMS)

If you ask, our staff can text you a link code instead of reading it out to you. Here's how that works:

  • We text only when you ask. Each text is a one-time message containing a link code, not marketing, and you get one text per request.
  • The message contains only the code and a link to core16.ca, which opens the app with the code filled in. It doesn't include the student's name, school, grade or anything about their marks.
  • We don't store your phone number. It's used to send that one message and isn't saved in our systems.
  • Our texting provider, Twilio, does see the number and the message. Twilio is based in the United States and keeps delivery records under its own privacy policy. [Confirm how long Twilio keeps message logs and bodies on our account settings.]
  • Reply STOP to stop receiving texts from our number, and START to resume. Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply.
  • Only Canadian and US mobile numbers can receive texts.

Keep a texted code private until you've used it. Whoever uses a code first can connect a phone to the student's record. If someone else has already used your code, the app tells you it no longer works, and our staff will cancel it and issue a new one. 7. Where information is stored

On our servers. Student records, eligibility results, the contact log and the access log are stored in a database hosted by DigitalOcean in Toronto, Canada.

On your phone. The app keeps a copy of the student's record on the device so it works offline, and syncs changes to our servers.

Calendar and reminders (optional, off by default). If you turn them on in the app's Profile screen:

  • The app adds upcoming assignments to a separate "Core16" calendar on your phone. It only ever changes that calendar, and doesn't send any calendar information to us.
  • The app schedules assignment reminders on your phone. These are created by the phone itself, not sent from our servers.

You can turn either off in the app, or in your phone's Settings.

10. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you what we hold about you or your student.
  • Correct anything that's wrong. Families can fix most details directly in the app, and our advisors can correct a student's name in our records. Advisor contact-log entries aren't edited. Instead, we add a correction that points to the original entry, and both stay visible, so the record stays accurate.
  • Withdraw consent and close the account. We archive the student's record: the app stops syncing, advisors no longer see the student in their day-to-day lists, and a phone can't reconnect without a new link code. The record itself is kept for the periods in section 9, so it can be restored if you come back, unless you also ask us to delete it.
  • Delete a student's information. We permanently delete everything we hold about the student: their profile, courses and marks, earlier versions of the record, eligibility results, the advisor contact log, the staff access log entries about them, consent records, link codes and device tokens. If they're the only student on the account, we also delete the account, including the parent or guardian's email address, billing status and consent records. This includes the record of advice we gave, so we can't later show what we told you. [Confirm with counsel.]

Once deleted, information can't be recovered by us. Two things remain: our staff access log keeps an entry recording that a student's information was deleted, when, and by whom, identified only by an internal number with no name or other details; and deleted information can stay in our hosting provider's database backups until they expire (section 9). The app keeps its own copy on your phone until you delete it there or remove the app.

To make a request, contact us (section 13). We'll answer within 30 days, and may need to confirm your identity and your relationship to the student first.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca) [or the BC Information and Privacy Commissioner, if BC's PIPA applies: confirm with counsel].

11. How we protect information

  • Records are hosted in Canada and encrypted in transit between the app, the advisor portal and our servers.
  • No passwords: staff sign in with passkeys, and phones connect with one-time codes and device tokens that we store only in scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Staff sessions expire, and every staff view of a student's record is logged.
  • Only Core16 advisors and administrators can see student records, and there are no shared staff logins.
  • If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, we'll notify the people affected and the Privacy Commissioner, as the law requires.

No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there's less to protect.

12. What Core16 is and isn't

Core16 isn't affiliated with, endorsed by or acting for the NCAA or its Eligibility Center. Our results are a tracking and advisory tool. Only the NCAA Eligibility Center can certify a student's academic eligibility.
