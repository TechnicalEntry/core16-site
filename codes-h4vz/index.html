Core16 Demo Codes

Gets link codes for the four Core16 demo students, ready to type into the app during a demo.

Download for Mac

Apple Silicon Mac · macOS 14 Sonoma or later

  1. Open Core16-Demo-Codes.pkg from your Downloads and follow the installer. It will ask for your Mac password.
  2. The installer adds Core16 Demo Codes to your Dock.
  3. Click New code next to a student. The first time, it asks for the admin token Graham sent you. Paste it in and click Save. It won’t ask again.
  4. In Core16, open the Advisor tab, type or paste the code, and tap Link this phone.
