Why a hockey timetable needs watching

The schedule flexes around ice time. The NCAA's rules don't.

A player who finds out in Grade 12 has no way back. Core16 is built to catch three things early, while a course can still be swapped.

0units

Some courses count for nothing

Applied, open-level and workplace courses are what a player gets steered into when the timetable has to flex around practice. NCAA names them, and they add nothing to the 16.

MBF3CMEL3EWorkplace Mathematics 11

4a year

Sixteen over four years is four a year

NCAA sets no yearly load, but the arithmetic does. A light Grade 9 or 10 fills the 16 slowly and says nothing, until there's no room left in Grade 12. Core16 shows the pace, and says plainly it's arithmetic, not a rule.

85%= 4 or 3

The same mark is worth different points

An 85 is a 4 in Ontario and a 3 in British Columbia. BC's top band starts at 86, so an 84 reads as a strong mark and is worth no more than a 73. Every conversion goes through the student's province.

Try it

One mark, two provinces

Drag the mark. Core16 converts it to NCAA quality points the way each province's outline does, and shows how close the mark sits to the next band. That nearness is what the app calls a cliff, and it only ever shows it for a course still in progress, where the mark can still move.

The BC 86 is the highest-value number in the app. One more point, one more quality point.

For the player

A phone that knows where you stand, written for a 15-year-old.

The iPhone app is the record. Courses and marks go in off the report card, it works offline against NCAA's list, and it keeps the hockey log in the same place as the due dates.

  • 01

    Overview, with no verdict

    The core courses counted, core GPA against the 2.3 minimum, the next two due dates and the nearest cliff. The app never says "on track" as one word, because the Eligibility Center is the only one who can.

  • 02

    Add a course, get the answer straight away

    Search the list for your grade and province, or type one the list doesn't have. A course NCAA won't count says so before it's on the timetable, not after the credit is earned.

  • 03

    Marks, due dates and reminders

    Log assignments and the report-card mark. Due dates carry a flag that gets louder as the day comes, and can mirror into a Core16 calendar with an alert the night before.

  • 04

    Where to focus

    A course three points from the next band gets a nudge showing the exact core GPA that reaching it would give. Two core courses in one subject at a C or below gets flagged as a pattern worth getting help with.

  • 05

    Hockey hours beside the homework

    Practices, games, skills and off-ice sessions, with weekly and season hours and a heads-up when a due date lands on a heavy stretch of ice.

  1. Step 1

    The phone holds the record

    Courses, marks and due dates go in on the player's iPhone, off the report card, and it works offline. Nothing in Core16 is computed anywhere else first.

  2. Step 2

    Core16 checks it against NCAA's own list

    Each course is matched to the Eligibility Center's outline for the province, Ontario by code, BC by title. Marks become quality points on the province's bands. Approved courses fill the 16; excluded ones are named; anything unassessed holds back the count.

  3. Step 3

    Everyone who looks after the player reads the same figures

    The phone syncs to Core16, and the same record appears on the parent's page, the player's Today page at school, and the advisor's roster. A change made anywhere else waits for the phone, so there is one record and no quiet edits.

Grade 11 and 12 decisions

How long to play junior is now a date.

Under the NCAA's 2026 age rules, a player's five years of eligibility start the fall after their 19th birthday, or when they first enrol in college, whichever comes first. A birthday before 1 September starts it that fall; on or after, the next one.

Core16 works out the latest fall a player's clock can start from a birth month and year, and how many seasons that leaves after Grade 12 before it does. It's shown as a date, in a neutral colour, and it never touches the course figures.

What Core16 won't say

A confident wrong answer is the failure this product exists to prevent.

Core16 tracks and flags. The NCAA Eligibility Center is the only certifier, and nothing on any screen pretends otherwise.

Never "certified", never "eligible"

Those are the Eligibility Center's words. Core16 says approved, won't count, or needs a look, and shows the 16 and the GPA that the Center will eventually see.

No green while a course is unsettled

A course NCAA hasn't assessed blocks the count however good the numbers look. The app and the portal hold the warning colour until an advisor or the school has settled it.

AVI4M: needs a look, so it holds back the 16

Figures round away from the line

A mark or GPA beside a threshold rounds down and the distance to the next band rounds up, so a student a hair under the minimum is never shown over it.

2.296 reads 2.29, 2.30, beside "Below 2.3"

An entered mark beats a computed one

A report-card mark supersedes the app's own assignment average, because it reflects everything the teacher counted. Three logged assignments out of twelve is not a GPA.
